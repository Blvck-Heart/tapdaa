Our Process

We work tirelessly to design the best experience, and we want to guide you step by step on the beautiful journey of using Tapdaa to create that experience together. If you choose to use Tapdaa, here's how you'll navigate the process.

  1. You decide on the suitable tag and service.
  2. You place the order for the tag you requested.
  3. We will contact you for further details.
  4. The service contents you wish to use will be set up.
  5. The specified tags are prepared.
  6. The tags are shipped to your business.
